The US Commission on International Religious Freedom (USCIRF) is an independent, bipartisan federal government commission created by the International Religious Freedom Act of 1998 that monitors religious freedom violations abroad and makes policy recommendations to the President, Secretary of State, and Congress. USCIRF publishes annual reports designating Countries of Particular Concern (CPCs), Special Watch List countries, and Entities of Particular Concern.

## Features

| Name | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Annual Report | Comprehensive annual report documenting religious freedom conditions in countries worldwide, with CPC and SWL designations and policy recommendations. |
| Countries of Particular Concern Designations | Formal designation recommendations for countries that engage in or tolerate particularly severe violations of religious freedom. |
| Special Watch List | Countries that do not meet the CPC threshold but require close monitoring due to severe violations. |
| Entities of Particular Concern | Non-state actor designations for groups that engage in particularly severe violations of religious freedom abroad. |
| Country Reports and Fact Sheets | Detailed country-by-country assessments of religious freedom conditions and minority group situations. |
| Policy Briefs and Recommendations | Targeted policy recommendations for the President, Secretary of State, and Congress. |
